Her story

Leadership, with a human centre.

Her work moves between the boardroom and the community — connecting investment with opportunity, public office with service, and ambition with the people it should lift.

As Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Kaduna Investment and Finance Company, Barr. Francesca Naymarie Musa-Akande leads with a conviction that capital is most meaningful when it builds confidence, creates pathways, and strengthens the future of communities.

That same belief carries into her advocacy for young entrepreneurs, women and girls, community wellbeing, and inclusive civic participation. Each chapter is different; the purpose remains constant.
