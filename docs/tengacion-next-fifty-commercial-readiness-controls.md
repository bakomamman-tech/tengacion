# Tengacion Next Fifty Commercial Readiness Controls

## Purpose

This implementation extends Tengacion's governed External Readiness operating system with the next 50 consecutive software work packages after CAPITAL-011. It reuses the existing evidence, owner-attestation, independent-review, expiry, dependency, audit-history, controlled-sharing, and revocation controls instead of creating a parallel authority.

The first 40 packages are traced directly to the checked-in Capital, Distribution, and Revenue 30/60/90 roadmaps. The final 10 packages form the post-revenue Commercial Maturity extension so the requested batch contains 50 complete, reviewable contracts.

Software readiness is not a real-world outcome. These workflows record evidence and support human review; they do not authorize fundraising, investor or partner outreach, pricing changes, contracts, discounts, payouts, refunds, account restrictions, risk acceptance, spending, or money movement.

## Commercial maturity extension

### COMMERCIAL-001 — Build Profitability And Contribution Margin Scorecard

Separate gross volume from contribution economics using only known payment, creator-share, refund, dispute, support, moderation, infrastructure, partner, and Akuso cost evidence. Missing costs remain explicit gaps.

### COMMERCIAL-002 — Measure Revenue Concentration And Dependency Risk

Measure aggregate concentration across creator cohorts, categories, channels, partners, sponsors, markets, and payment providers. The workflow surfaces dependency risk but does not terminate or renegotiate relationships.

### COMMERCIAL-003 — Establish Pricing Fairness And Value Review

Review price, fee, discount, promotion, creator-earnings, fan-value, refund, complaint, and conversion evidence across comparable aggregate cohorts. Final pricing remains a human commercial decision.

### COMMERCIAL-004 — Review Creator And Fan Value Distribution

Review creator earnings, repeat earnings, payout health, fan spend, benefit delivery, retention, refunds, complaints, and support together so marketplace growth does not optimize one side at the expense of the other.

### COMMERCIAL-005 — Improve Cash Conversion And Working Capital Visibility

Track collection timing, payout timing, refund and dispute reserves, partner receivables, vendor commitments, aging, and runway confidence without moving, reserving, or approving funds.

### COMMERCIAL-006 — Validate Partner And Channel Margin Quality

Compare partner and channel reach with creator share, fees, campaign cost, support, moderation, delivery, refunds, disputes, and Akuso cost before scale or renewal recommendations.

### COMMERCIAL-007 — Reduce Support And Operating Cost Per Revenue Workflow

Measure cost and service quality across purchase, subscription, payout-support, partner-delivery, and refund workflows. Efficiency recommendations cannot remove safety, finance, trust, or human-review gates.

### COMMERCIAL-008 — Detect Commercial Leakage, Abuse, And Promotion Risk

Surface evidence-backed leakage and abuse hypotheses across refunds, disputes, chargebacks, promotions, referrals, entitlements, partner reporting, and payouts. Weak signals cannot automatically accuse, block, or restrict users or funds.

### COMMERCIAL-009 — Measure Akuso Commercial ROI And Cost Discipline

Compare Akuso workflow cost with helpful outcomes, fallbacks, refusals, unsupported claims, support deflection, creator value, partner value, and safety quality. Model or vendor changes remain human-approved.

### COMMERCIAL-010 — Decide The Next Commercial Growth Path

Compose profitability, concentration, pricing fairness, marketplace value, cash conversion, margin, operating cost, leakage, Akuso ROI, retention, and trust evidence into a reversible human decision about the next commercial focus.

## Authority boundaries

- Existing payment, payout, entitlement, refund, moderation, account, partner-access, audit, and external-readiness authorities remain canonical.
- Every new readiness record requires evidence coverage, current evidence, owner attestation, expiry, and independent review before it can become approved.
- External sharing remains recipient-bound, sanitized, expiring, revocable, and invalidated by stale evidence or record revision.
- Decision records are advisory governance records. They do not execute commercial, financial, legal, trust, safety, or account actions.
- Akuso may draft, explain, summarize, retrieve approved context, and route work. It may not approve pricing, contracts, payouts, refunds, financing, risk acceptance, moderation, account actions, or money movement.

- The next-fifty module must contain exactly 4 Capital, 18 Distribution, 18 Revenue, and 10 Commercial packages.
- Every package must trace to a checked-in roadmap or this control document, have non-empty requirements, and have non-empty acceptance criteria.
- Existing External Readiness authentication, evidence freshness, dependency, independent-review, revision invalidation, sanitized sharing, and audit-log regressions must continue to pass.
