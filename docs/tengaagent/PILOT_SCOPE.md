# TengaAgent Controlled Pilot Scope

The controlled pilot validates the currently built TengaAgent channels and workflows before production payment collection and before a production merge.

In scope:

- published tenant web agent;
- tenant knowledge and grounded AI replies;
- lead capture and appointment requests;
- human handoff;
- owner inboxes and follow-up operations;
- plan entitlements and monthly conversation enforcement;
- WhatsApp inbound/outbound AI messaging for eligible tenants;
- WhatsApp voice-note transcription for eligible tenants;
- existing Google/Microsoft availability integrations when configured;
- readiness, audit and operational checks.

Out of scope until later milestones:

- charging customers through Paystack/Stripe subscriptions;
- automatic production rollout;
- telephone/PSTN AI calls;
- relaxing tenant or provider security checks for convenience.
